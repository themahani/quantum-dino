THE SCIENCE UNDER THE PIXELS

Not just a quantum dice roll.

Two interference experiments per altitude begin in a predictable state. An environment qubit introduces a controlled phase-flip channel. As noise grows, the interference that favors 00 fades—and the terrain stops keeping its rhythm.

Collect floating diamonds for +100 points. Their heights vary above the cacti; higher diamonds demand noisier jumps. Small jumps are safer. The scene accelerates as you survive.

Waiting for circuit results.

Displayed bars are measured marginals; outlined bars are the analytic ideal-channel reference, not a second job. Device noise may change the measured results. Classical sampling from the cached distributions drives gameplay; the quantum backend is not contacted during play.

Replays reuse this seed and the same quantum table. Identical inputs reproduce the world; different jumps can change its future.
